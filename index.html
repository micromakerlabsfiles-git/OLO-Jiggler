Installation Manual

1
Connect Board:
Plug the ESP32-C3 Super Mini into your computer.
2
Initialize:
Click 'Connect' in the Firmware Flasher box below.
3
Select Port:
Select the COM port labeled "USB Serial Device" or "CH343".
4
Install:
Click Install. Wait ~60s until the success message appears.

Wire your components exactly as listed below:

Prerequisites
  • Browser: Chrome, Edge, Brave, or Opera on a desktop computer. Safari and Firefox do not support Web Serial.
  • Cable: A USB-C cable capable of data transfer (not just a charging cable).
  • Connectivity: Your host computer must have Bluetooth enabled to connect to the Jiggler once installed.
