OLO Jiggler Installer

BLE Mouse Jiggler with Display by Micromaker Labs

Installation Manual

1
Connect Board:
Plug the ESP32-C3 Super Mini into your computer's USB port.
2
Initialize:
Click the "Connect" button in the device panel below.
3
Select Port:
In the browser popup, select the COM port labeled "USB Serial Device" or "CH343".
4
Install:
Click "Install OLO Jiggler". Wait ~60s until the success message appears.

Device Connection

Firmware Version: 1.0.0
Architecture: ESP32-C3 Super Mini

System Terminal

Hardware & Wiring Guide

Important: This firmware is strictly compiled for the ESP32-C3 Super Mini. It will not work on standard ESP32 or ESP8266 boards.

Wire your components exactly as listed below before powering on the device:

Component ESP32-C3 Super Mini Pin
OLED SDA GPIO 20
OLED SCL GPIO 21
Passive Buzzer GPIO 2
TTP233 Sensor GPIO 1
Prerequisites
  • Browser: Chrome, Edge, Brave, or Opera on desktop.
  • Cable: A USB-C cable capable of data transfer.
