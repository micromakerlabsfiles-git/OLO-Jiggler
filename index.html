OLO Jiggler Installer

Official Firmware Flasher by Micromaker Labs

Prerequisites

  • Supported Browser: Use Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Brave on a desktop computer (Safari/Firefox are not supported).
  • Data Cable: A USB-C cable capable of data transfer (not just charging).
  • Hardware: An ESP32-C3 Super Mini board wired according to the OLO schematic.

Installation Manual

1 Connect the Board: Plug the ESP32-C3 into your computer's USB port.
2 Initialize Flasher: Click the "Connect" button in the panel on the right.
3 Select Port: A browser popup will appear. Select the COM port labeled "USB Serial Device" or "CH343" and click Connect.
4 Install: Click "Install OLO Bit". The process takes about 60 seconds. Do not unplug the device until the success message appears.

Device Connection
